The app I built

The collection outgrew a spreadsheet, so I built Adcock Botanical Garden — a proper catalogue. Every specimen has an accession number, a location, a photographic timeline and a care history, the way a real botanical garden keeps records.

  • Identification by AI. Point it at a photo and it suggests which of my own plants it is, or what species it might be.
  • Health checks from photographs. It reads a plant against how that species should look, and flags rot, pests or sun damage.
  • Watering, by area. Water a whole yard in one tap, or a single pot.
  • Frost and heat warnings. Wired to the forecast — a cold night produces a list of what needs bringing in, ordered as a walk through the garden.

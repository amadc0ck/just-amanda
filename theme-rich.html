What each one does

Ground

Alternating section grounds, the wash, then a lavender tint under the journal. White cards on an ivory ground are two shades of the same non-colour; give the section a tone and the cards get an edge. The cheapest of the four and probably the one doing the most.

Tint

One colour per obsession, falling from the top of each card and fading out by 60% so text still sits on paper. The top bar takes the card's own colour instead of terracotta for all five. Uses sage, lavender, amethyst and gold, four colours currently almost absent.

Depth

Sage taken down to #4C5B3E (same hue, no longer a pastel) as the footer ground. The badge gets a cream disc so it does not sink into the green, and is nudged 1.6% down because its ink sits high in the master PNG.

Wash

An illustration run large and faint behind a section, as a ground rather than a picture, here ornament-amethyst.png, the crystal, with the amethyst divider above it. You own six pieces of purple artwork and the live site shows none of them. Decorative only: aria-hidden, never under body copy.
